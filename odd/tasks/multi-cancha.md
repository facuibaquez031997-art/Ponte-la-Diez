Objective: (1) the chat button of "Explorar canchas" sends a ready message with the court and the chosen slot; (2) an owner can say how many courts of a size they have, and the court then offers that many simultaneous slots per hour.
Branch: feat/multi-cancha (from feat/tamano-cancha, because it builds on the size of the court) · Delivery: ask-on-risk · Test runner: vitest (`npm test` in apps/server), client: `tsc -b` + eslint (no client test runner)

## Specs

- S1 Ready message from "Explorar canchas", user words (L1): "como jugador cuando estoy en /canchas en explorar canchas y selecciono el horario y pongo chatear con el dueño. cuando apreto el boton lo ideal seria que se mande un msj predeterminado de ultima para que el jugador haga la consulta que quiera pero con los datos de la cancha y el horario que el jugador selecciono." So: with a slot chosen, pressing the chat button opens the conversation AND sends a ready message with the court (name, size, barrio) and the chosen day and hour, so the owner already knows what it is about and the player writes whatever else they want. Assumption A1: without a chosen slot the button only opens the chat, as before (no ready message), so reopening the chat does not repeat greetings.
- S2 Several courts of the same size, user words (L1): "en el momento que el dueño esta editando para agregar la cancha la cantidad de jugadores para la que esta hecha. puede pasar que tengan un predio que tiene una cancha de once y esa la dividen en 3 canchas de 5 jugadores. entonces tiene que tener la opcion de poner la cantidad de canchas tiene de cada tamaño y en caso de que sea mas de 1 cancha entonces tiene la opcion de tener dos turnos en el mismo horario. por ejemplo: dos canchas de 5 jugadores: dos turnos disponibles de 18:00 a 19:00 y si un jugador reserva una cancha queda reservado un turno, pero el otro todavia esta disponible porque queda una cancha aun disponible." So a court (an entry of the owner, which already has a size) has a quantity (`cantidad`, 1 to 10, default 1): the owner enters one entry per size and says how many courts of that size there are. With a quantity of 2 there are two slots at the same hour; booking one leaves the other free. Assumptions: A2 the player sees one chip per hour with how many are free ("18:00 · 2 libres"); A3 booking an hour takes any free court of that hour: if the chosen one was just taken, the next free one is used, and only when none is left the booking is refused (409); A4 lowering the quantity removes only the FREE slots above the new quantity, never a booked one; A5 the agenda shows each court of the hour as its own row ("Cancha 1", "Cancha 2") when the quantity is more than 1; A6 an offer made in the chat is about a court and an hour: it stays bookable while any court of that hour is free.
- S3 NOT in this request, stated so nobody assumes it: the entries do not exclude each other. A venue that splits one court of 11 into 3 of 5 would register an entry of 11 (quantity 1) and an entry of 5 (quantity 3): renting the 11 does NOT block the 5s, and renting a 5 does not block the 11. It is listed as a pending item and asked to the programmer.
