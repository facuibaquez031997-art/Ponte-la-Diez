## Specs

- S1 The player chooses what to pay when booking, user words (L1): "que el usuario elija, la idea es que tenga la opcion rapida de abonar la seña para que le quede reservada la cancha. y despues termina de abonar lo que resta con el dueño de la cancha." So two options per booking: SEÑA (`Cancha.montoSena`) or TOTAL (`Cancha.precioHora`). With the seña, the remaining balance is paid at the court to the owner, outside the app.
- S2 Booking: a logged-in player books one FREE future slot (`POST /reservas`, body `turnoId` and the payment choice). It creates `Reserva` (PENDING, `montoTotal` = court `precioHora`) and `Pago` (PENDING, `monto` = what is charged now) and holds the slot (`Turno.estado` RESERVED) in one transaction; two players racing for the same slot: exactly one wins, the other gets 409. Assumption A1: a PENDING booking is held for 15 minutes; if it is not paid by then, or the payment is REJECTED, the hold is released (slot FREE again). Assumption A2: `Reserva.turnoId` is unique, so a released booking is deleted instead of kept as CANCELLED (no booking history for abandoned payments).
- S3 `POST /pagos/create-preference` (roadmap): creates the Mercado Pago preference for MY PENDING booking, with the amount from S1, and returns the checkout URL (`init_point`); stores `mpPreferenceId`. Only the booking's player can ask for it.
- S4 `POST /pagos/webhook` (roadmap, "procesar IPN y confirmar reservas de forma automática"): public endpoint; the notification body is never trusted, the server re-reads the payment from the Mercado Pago API and checks the signature (`x-signature` with `MP_WEBHOOK_SECRET`); idempotent (the same notification twice changes nothing); approved -> `Pago` APPROVED (+ `mpPaymentId`) + `Reserva` CONFIRMED, only if the amount paid is not lower than the amount charged; any other status (rejected, pending, cancelled) changes nothing. Changed 2026-11-14 (see Log): the first draft released the hold on a rejected payment, but Checkout Pro lets the player retry with another card, so the hold now just ends by itself after 15 minutes (A1).
- S5 A player reads their bookings (`GET /reservas/mine`): slot, court, state, amount paid and amount still to pay at the court (`montoTotal` minus paid).
- S6 Client flow: free-slot chips in "Explorar canchas" become bookable; the player picks seña or total, is sent to Mercado Pago, and comes back to a result page (approved / pending / rejected) using the `back_urls`; "Mis reservas" lists the bookings (S5). Stitch/Figma reference is checked before building any UI.
- S7 Mercado Pago credentials only in `.env` (`MP_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `MP_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, public URL for the webhook), never in the repo; the server starts without them (booking endpoints answer a clear error until they are set).
- S8 Owner notification, user words (L4): "en el dashboard del dueño no aparece ninguna notificacion de las canchas que estan confirmadas". When a booking of one of their courts becomes CONFIRMED the owner gets an inbox notification (the same bell as the players) and a push. Assumption A3: sent once per booking (a repeated webhook does not repeat it); a failure to notify never makes the webhook fail; the text says who booked, which court, day and hour, what was paid and what is left to collect at the court; tapping it opens the owner bookings page (S9). Needs `Notificacion.reservaId`.
- S9 Owner bookings list: the owner sees the bookings of THEIR courts (`GET /reservas/owner`, `@Roles('OWNER')`) in a page of the owner panel (`/dueno/reservas`, linked from the menu): PENDING (waiting for payment) and CONFIRMED, with the player's name and photo, court, day and hour, what was paid and what is left to collect at the court. Assumption A4: the player's phone and email are NOT shown (privacy, see pending #26); the owner talks to the player through the chat of S10.
- S10 Chat with the owner, user words (L4, L5): "tiene que tener la opcion de estar comunicandose con el dueño de la cancha que se que esta como pendiente tambien" and, in L5, "dejemos lo del chat con el dueño aparte diseñandolo ya colgado en la reserva". DESIGN ONLY in this feature (`docs/chat-por-reserva.md`): the conversation hangs from the booking. NOT implemented here.
- S11 Owner cancels a booking, user words (L7): "es posible que exista la posibilidad de que ningun jugador pueda ir a jugar, entonces debe existir la posibilidad de que el dueño de la cancha en ese caso cancele el turno, ese turno cancelado vuelva a estar disponible por si otro jugador desea alquilar la cancha. y el dueño le haga la devolucion del dinero al usuario que se comunico con el dueño de la cancha de que cancelaba." The owner of the court cancels a booking (paid or still pending) that has not started (`POST /reservas/:id/cancelar`, `@Roles('OWNER')`); the slot goes back to FREE and can be booked again; the refund is made BY THE OWNER outside the app (Assumption A5: the app only records it, it does not move money, and the player does not cancel from the app). The player gets a notification.
- S12 History of the owner, user words (L6): "el historial de reservas programadas, canceladas o vencidas deberian aparecer en el dashboard del dueño para que controle sus ganancias y como concurren los usuarios con su cancha". A booking that stops existing (expired unpaid or cancelled by the owner) leaves a history record (who, court, hour, total, what was paid, payment id, reason, when). Assumption A6: separate table, because `Reserva.turnoId` is unique and a kept row would block rebooking the slot; the booking itself is still deleted.
- S13 Refund tracking: a cancelled booking that had been paid is "devolución pendiente" until the owner marks it as done (`PATCH /reservas/historial/:id/devolucion`); the dashboard shows what was collected, what is pending to return, what was returned and the net, plus how many bookings are confirmed, expired and cancelled.
- S14 Notifications carry the screen they open (`Notificacion.url`), so the player's cancellation notice opens "Mis reservas" and the owner's one opens the owner bookings.
- Not included (not requested): refunds and cancellation policy (pending #11), owner payments dashboard (S10 of canchas-turnos), paying the remaining balance in the app, cleaning up other players' abandoned holds with a cron (A1 releases on read/webhook; revisit if needed).
