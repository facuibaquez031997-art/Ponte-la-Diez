Objective: Fase 3 - players book a free slot (Reserva) and pay the seña or the full price through Mercado Pago Checkout Pro; a webhook confirms the booking automatically.
Branch: feat/pagos-mercadopago · Delivery: ask-on-risk, chain strategy chosen: feature-branch-chain (slice boundaries recorded in the Log) · Test runner: vitest (`npm test` in apps/server), client: `tsc -b` + eslint (no client test runner)

## Specs

- S1 The player chooses what to pay when booking, user words (L1): "que el usuario elija, la idea es que tenga la opcion rapida de abonar la seña para que le quede reservada la cancha. y despues termina de abonar lo que resta con el dueño de la cancha." So two options per booking: SEÑA (`Cancha.montoSena`) or TOTAL (`Cancha.precioHora`). With the seña, the remaining balance is paid at the court to the owner, outside the app.
- S2 Booking: a logged-in player books one FREE future slot (`POST /reservas`, body `turnoId` and the payment choice). It creates `Reserva` (PENDING, `montoTotal` = court `precioHora`) and `Pago` (PENDING, `monto` = what is charged now) and holds the slot (`Turno.estado` RESERVED) in one transaction; two players racing for the same slot: exactly one wins, the other gets 409. Assumption A1: a PENDING booking is held for 15 minutes; if it is not paid by then, or the payment is REJECTED, the hold is released (slot FREE again). Assumption A2: `Reserva.turnoId` is unique, so a released booking is deleted instead of kept as CANCELLED (no booking history for abandoned payments).
- S3 `POST /pagos/create-preference` (roadmap): creates the Mercado Pago preference for MY PENDING booking, with the amount from S1, and returns the checkout URL (`init_point`); stores `mpPreferenceId`. Only the booking's player can ask for it.
- S4 `POST /pagos/webhook` (roadmap, "procesar IPN y confirmar reservas de forma automática"): public endpoint; the notification body is never trusted, the server re-reads the payment from the Mercado Pago API and checks the signature (`x-signature` with `MP_WEBHOOK_SECRET`); idempotent (the same notification twice changes nothing); approved -> `Pago` APPROVED (+ `mpPaymentId`) + `Reserva` CONFIRMED, only if the amount paid is not lower than the amount charged; any other status (rejected, pending, cancelled) changes nothing. Changed 2026-11-14 (see Log): the first draft released the hold on a rejected payment, but Checkout Pro lets the player retry with another card, so the hold now just ends by itself after 15 minutes (A1).
- S5 A player reads their bookings (`GET /reservas/mine`): slot, court, state, amount paid and amount still to pay at the court (`montoTotal` minus paid).
- S6 Client flow: free-slot chips in "Explorar canchas" become bookable; the player picks seña or total, is sent to Mercado Pago, and comes back to a result page (approved / pending / rejected) using the `back_urls`; "Mis reservas" lists the bookings (S5). Stitch/Figma reference is checked before building any UI.
- S7 Mercado Pago credentials only in `.env` (`MP_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `MP_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, public URL for the webhook), never in the repo; the server starts without them (booking endpoints answer a clear error until they are set).
- Not included (not requested): refunds and cancellation policy (pending #11), owner payments dashboard (S10 of canchas-turnos), paying the remaining balance in the app, cleaning up other players' abandoned holds with a cron (A1 releases on read/webhook; revisit if needed).
