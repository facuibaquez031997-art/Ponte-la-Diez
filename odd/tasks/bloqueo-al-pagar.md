Objective: (1) the shared space only blocks the linked courts once the payment is confirmed; (2) the link between courts can be about ONE specific court (an entry with five courts of 11 where only one of them is divided into 3 of 5, and the others stay pure).
Branch: feat/bloqueo-al-pagar (from feat/canchas-compartidas, because it changes how that feature blocks) · Delivery: ask-on-risk · Test runner: vitest (`npm test` in apps/server), client: `tsc -b` + eslint (no client test runner)

## Specs

- S1 Blocking starts when the payment is confirmed, user words (L1): "que el bloqueo comience cuando se confirma el pago. cuando se cobra la seña tambien que se bloquee el turno." So: a booking that still waits for its payment does NOT block the courts that share the space (it still holds its own slot for 15 minutes, as always); once the seña or the full price is approved (the booking is CONFIRMED) it blocks them. Assumptions: A1 both the seña and the full payment confirm; A2 the booking keeps its own slot while unpaid, so two players cannot pay for the very same slot.
- S2 What happens when two players pay for linked courts at the same time. Since the hold no longer blocks the other court, two players can both be paying. The first payment that is confirmed wins; for the second one, whose payment Mercado Pago already approved, the booking is NOT confirmed: it is closed, the slot is freed, and the money paid is recorded as a refund pending to that player (like when the owner cancels), the player is told, and the owner sees it in "Vencidas y canceladas" with the new reason "Espacio ocupado". Assumption A3: the refund is made by the owner outside the app, as for the other refunds (pending #35, #36). Also A4: a player who has not paid yet and asks for the payment link of a booking whose space was already confirmed by a linked court is told it is no longer available (409), so they do not pay in vain.
- S3 The link is about specific courts, user words (L1): "cada dueño de cancha puede tener predios muy grandes, en las que pueden entrar como por ejemplo 5 canchas de 11 jugadores. tiene que tener la opcion de agregar la cantidad de canchas de 11 que necesite si asi lo tenga en su predio y ademas que al marcar que si comparten canchas de 5 o de 7 o de 9 y si se aceptan turnos en dicha cancha que se bloqueen los turnos de la cancha de once. pero que al hacer click de que se confirma que comparten cancha no influyan en otras que haya registrado. osea puede tener 2 canchas de 11 jugadores y una cancha se divide en 3 canchas de 5 jugadores y otra puede ser pura y exclusivamente cancha de 11 jugadores nada mas." So: an entry already has a quantity (up to 10: "5 canchas de 11" already works); the link says which court of this entry shares the space with which court of the other entry (or "todas"). Example: an entry of 11 with 2 courts and an entry of 5 with 3 courts; the court number 1 of the 11 is divided into the 3 of 5. Booking a 5 (confirmed) blocks ONLY the court 1 of the 11, so the court 2 of the 11 stays bookable; booking the court 1 of the 11 blocks the three of 5; booking the court 2 of the 11 blocks nothing. A link is stored as two numbers: the court of this entry (0 = all of them) and the court of the other entry (0 = all of them); the links that already exist mean "all with all", so nothing changes for them. Assumptions: A5 if the owner has two divisible courts of 11, it makes two entries of 5 (each linked to its own court of 11); A6 lowering the quantity of an entry removes the links that pointed to the courts that no longer exist; A7 links are not transitive: an 11 divided into 5s and also into 7s needs each pair linked.
- S4 Booking takes a free court that is not blocked: asking for a court of 11 that is blocked by a 5 gives the player another free court of 11 of that hour if there is one (the other court of the entry), and only when every free court of the hour is blocked the answer is "el espacio está ocupado" (409).
